### Patterns & Conventions

- **Notebook versioning**: `train_generation_v3.ipynb` — never overwrite v1/v2 from Story 4.3
- **Code reuse**: Maximize reuse of `data_loader.py` and training infrastructure from Story 4.3
- **Python naming**: `snake_case` for files, `PascalCase` for classes
- **Type hints**: `X | Y` union syntax (Python 3.10+)
- **Docstrings**: Google-style on all public methods
- **Config-driven**: Hyperparameters in `generation_config.yaml`
- **Reproducibility**: Same seed (42), same dataset, same split ratios as Story 4.3

### Tech Stack

- **Python** >=3.10
- **PyTorch** >=2.0.0 — model training and inference
- **transformers** (Hugging Face) — Gemma 4 2B model loading, tokenizer
- **peft** — LoRA/QLoRA adapter configuration
- **bitsandbytes** — 4-bit quantization (NF4)
- **trl** — `SFTTrainer` for supervised fine-tuning
- **accelerate** — mixed precision training
- **GPU requirement**: Colab T4 (~5GB VRAM with QLoRA 4-bit) or local GPU ≥8GB

### Notes from Previous Stories

- Story 4.3 `data_loader.py` provides shared data loading — reuse directly, do not duplicate
- Story 4.3 JSONL dataset in `data/training/` — use identical dataset for fair comparison
- Story 4.3 instruction templates in `generation_config.yaml` — reuse same templates
- Gemma 4 2B uses different chat template format than Qwen2-1.5B — tokenization must adapt
- Gemma 4 2B is ~0.5B params larger → expect slightly higher VRAM (~5GB vs ~4GB) and slower training
- Batch size may need reduction (2 vs 4) to fit within Colab T4 VRAM — compensate with higher gradient_accumulation_steps
